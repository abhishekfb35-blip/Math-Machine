## Why this instantly saves your design layout:

   1. Ditches the Box-in-a-Box: It uses a flat card structure that seamlessly flows directly down your cart interface column instead of sitting in an aggressive dark green container box.
   2. Proper Spacing Protection: The tracking labels use a responsive @media query trick. If the screen shrinks down onto a smaller iPhone or Android viewport, the text sizing drops slightly to ensure it never overlaps awkwardly.
   3. Clean Visual Anchor: The color scheme is locked strictly to your brand's natural premium teal green, utilizing a tiny splash of tracking orange solely to draw the shopper's eye straight to their active target tier.
