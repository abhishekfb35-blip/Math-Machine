To track this exact customer journey, you need an E-commerce Funnel and Abandonment Report. You can build this by combining data from your web analytics tool (like Google Analytics 4) and your backend database/CRM. [1] 
Here is the exact structure and the specific metrics you need for your report:
## 1. Traffic and Reach (How many came?)

* Total Visitors: Total unique users who landed on your website.
* Traffic Sources: Where they came from (e.g., Google, Instagram, direct link). [2, 3] 

## 2. Funnel Progression (Till what point did they reach?)

* Product Views: How many users looked at a specific item.
* Cart Adds: How many users clicked "Add to Cart."
* Checkout Initiated: How many users reached the first page of checkout. [4, 5] 

## 3. Intent and Attempt (What did they try to order?)

* Cart Contents: Item names, sizes, colors, and quantities left in the cart.
* Cart Value: The potential total revenue of those unpurchased items. [6, 7] 

## 4. Success Rates (Were they successful?)

* Completed Orders: Total successful transactions.
* Conversion Rate: The percentage of total visitors who actually bought something. [8, 9] 

## 5. Friction and Drop-offs (What issues did they face?)

* Payment Failures: Erroneous card attempts or declined UPI/wallet transactions.
* Error Logs: Technical glitches, slow loading times, or broken promo codes.
* Form Drop-offs: Users who quit because checkout forms asked for too much information.

## 6. Lead Capture (Did they leave contact info or register?) [10] 

* Account Creations: Number of users who registered for a permanent account.
* Guest Checkout Emails: Contact details typed into the checkout form before quitting.
* Newsletter Sign-ups: Users who opted in via pop-ups but did not buy.

## 7. Recovery Action (Did we get back to them?)

* Cart Recovery Emails Sent: Automated emails triggered after abandonment.
* Recovery Open/Click Rates: How many users opened those emails and clicked back to the site.
* SMS/WhatsApp Triggers: Text-based recovery alerts sent to mobile numbers.

## 8. Support and Assistance (Did we offer help?)

* Live Chat Interactions: How many users opened your site's chat box.
* Assisted Conversions: Sales completed after a support agent talked to the customer. [11]
