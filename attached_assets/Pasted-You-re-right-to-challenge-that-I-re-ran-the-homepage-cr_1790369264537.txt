## What I would investigate now instead

The interesting question is no longer:

**"Can Google/AI crawl the homepage?"**

It clearly can.

The better question is:

**"Is the homepage giving Google/AI enough *entity and topical context* to understand TurtleLittle beyond a collection of products?"**

That's a much more nuanced issue.

And from the fresh crawl, I'd say **you're doing better than I initially gave you credit for**, but there are still some opportunities.

For example, your homepage currently establishes:

**TurtleLittle**
→ personalised luxury towels
→ bathrobes
→ blankets
→ kids
→ couples
→ cotton bathrobes
→ personalised products

That's good.

But it doesn't yet explicitly establish some potentially valuable concepts such as:

* **embroidered personalisation**
* **100% cotton terry**
* **personalised gifting**
* **India**
* **kids + adults + couples**
* **birthday / return gifting / wedding / anniversary**
* **why TurtleLittle's personalisation is different**
* **how the personalisation works**

Those are **content/entity opportunities**, not crawlability problems.

And I would **not start stuffing all of that onto the homepage**. Some belong on category pages, About, Personalisation, FAQ, etc.
